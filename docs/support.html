Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?
Open the app → Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete Account. This permanently erases all your health, diet, and body data.

How do I export my data?
Open the app → Settings ▸ Account ▸ Export Data. You can export in JSON or CSV format.

The app says my session has expired — what do I do?
Sign out from Settings and sign back in. If the problem persists, email us.

Is Empirical Tracker a medical device?
No. The app is a personal decision-support tool — it shows correlations, not causes, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician about your health.
